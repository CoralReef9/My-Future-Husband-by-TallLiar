My Future Husband: Chapter 3

by TallLiar

"And then just when I thought all the chaos was over, Balloon somehow got locked in his own room again!"

Paper chuckled to himself as OJ rambled on about one of his more stressful days he'd had managing the hotel recently, rolling his eyes as he shook his head.

"Wow, again? Someone must really have it out for him, huh?"

OJ shrugged at that, shifting about the newly acquired grocery bag in his arms with a quick huff before speaking again.

"Eh, maybe… I've heard that Nickel in particular really hates him. But I also wouldn't be surprised if he were doing this to himself, if I'm being honest."

The pair was just coming back from their quick trip to the grocery store, buying anything and everything that was in short supply at the hotel.

But even though everything seemed perfectly fine, and he should be feeling grateful that the two of them were finally spending time together after how busy OJ'd been recently… Paper couldn't help but feel slightly concerned about everything.

He hadn't been able to shake the feeling that something was… wrong with OJ.

For whatever reason, he hadn't been able to find OJ anywhere in the hotel that entire afternoon. He wasn't working in his office, or patrolling the halls, or even attempting (and failing) to cook the residents dinner again - it was almost like he'd completely vanished without a trace.

None of the other residents seemed to know where he'd gone either, but most of them just shrugged off his concerns. Some thought that OJ might've just been taking a walk outside - to get some fresh air, after spending so many days cramped up in his office working - but years of being OJ's friend was enough to tell Paper that the hotel manager simply didn't work like that.

OJ wouldn't dare leave his prized building for any sort of leisurely activity unless Paper actively pried him from that desk. If it weren't for his impressive physical strength and persuasion skills, it'd be absolutely impossible to get the glass out of his office on days he was cramped up there doing nothing but paperwork.

So finding him outside after all that was… certainly quite suspicious, to say the least. He maybe could've brushed it off if OJ simply explained what he'd actually been doing, but the glass simply refused to talk about where he'd been all day; and the more he thought about it, the less he believed his best friend's flimsy excuse about going grocery shopping (especially considering he'd been missing all day). He didn't even have a list or a bag to accompany him… he was almost certain OJ'd only said that so he could avoid the subject of where he'd been.

Why would he want to keep something like this from him? Did he not trust Paper enough to tell him? What kind of secret could he be hiding for him to be gone all day?

"…Paper? Hello? Earth to Paper?"

Paper's eyes snapped right back onto OJ the second he heard his friend calling his name, chuckling awkwardly to himself as he spoke.

"Oh, uh, sorry, I just… zoned out, is all."

"It's fine, I get that that sort of stuff happens sometimes. I was just worried for a second there."

The dorky smile that OJ gave him afterwards was enough to make Paper's heart skip a beat. Maybe a few dozen beats. There was just something about the way it seemed to glimmer in the moonlight, shimmering with the light of a thousand stars as if to remind Paper of how much he'd fallen so hopelessly in love with the hotel manager.

…Could anyone really blame him for that? When OJ was such a caring, hardworking object that always did his best to provide for those he cared about? When OJ sat down and listened to him, actually treating him like his own person and not someone to be ridiculed? When OJ showed him that no matter what happened, he'd stay by his side and continue to support him, never once making him feel like a heavy burden on the manager's shoulders? When with every passing moment, it became clearer and clearer that OJ truly valued him for who he was, and never pressured him into changing that for anyone in any way…

He let out a quiet sigh, shuffling the handle of his grocery bag so it'd rest on his shoulder instead and moving to hold his own hand so he'd refrain from reaching out for OJ's.

…Would he ever have a chance with OJ? Between the guy's hotel manager duties constantly clogging his schedule, his constant assurance that they were best friends, and the way he'd previously reacted to Salt's advances in terms of romance…

…It was hard for him to picture a future where he and OJ genuinely became a couple. It was hard for him to be confident that they'd ever be anything other than 'best friends'. Not when they'd been best friends for so long now…

…But maybe he'd learn to be fine with that. Perhaps he'd just bottle up his growing feelings for OJ for as long as he lived. Even if that fear of him missing his shot haunted the depths of his mind… maybe it was something he'd simply learn to live with.

He shook his head, trying to will his thoughts away as he glanced around his surroundings, looking for something else to be focused on. To be distracted by.

…It didn't take him long to find something… suspiciously out of place. Something different.

Among the thin blades of grass, innocently leaning against the vending machine in the distance, there lay some sort of… advanced contraption? Perhaps a fancy calculator? It was just… sitting there, like it'd been hastily abandoned by whoever had used it last. He was almost certain he hadn't seen that machine when he'd been tending to his garden yesterday.

He stared at it for a few seconds as he walked along the path back to the hotel, tempted to investigate it right then and there, but OJ then said something that made his heart practically leap into his throat.

"So uh… I was thinking… maybe we could take the rest of the day off? Y'know… to watch that show together? Like I mentioned we could earlier…"

Paper had almost completely forgotten that OJ'd suggested they watch Desperate Houses together after they go grocery shopping, the idea of doing so immediately sending his mind into overdrive.

Getting to spend quality time with OJ? Watching one of the shows he was extremely passionate about How could he possibly say no?

"Ah! Yes, of course, I'd love to watch some more episodes together!"

Of course, he was still somewhat suspicious of that machine, but he'd decided to push those thoughts aside for now. It wasn't every day that he and OJ were able to actually spend time together for non-work related purposes, after all…

Quality time with OJ was precious… spending time with OJ, no matter how long that time may be, was undeniably one of his absolute favourite things in the entire universe. He wouldn't dare waste even a second of it.

As the pair reached the doors to Hotel OJ, Paper took one last glance at the odd device leaning against the vending machine.

…Tomorrow.

If it was still there the next morning, he'd investigate it tomorrow.

-

Of course it was.

Of course the second his curiosity regarding the device he'd seen the night before reared its head once more, he just happened to spot it outside a window in the hallway he was walking through.

Paper sighed to himself, taking a quick glance at the door to OJ's office. As expected, it was locked shut, given that OJ was likely already working on whatever he had to do for the day… and he'd likely be working in there all day…

Surely nobody would notice if he quickly popped outside to give that device a look? After all, not many objects noticed him even when he was in the hotel… so who's to say whether it would even make all that much of a difference anyway?

…Yeah. It'll be fine. He'll only be out for a little while, and Soap could surely handle hotel maintenance until then.

With that thought, he began making his way towards the hotel's doors, sneakily striding past Pickle on the couch and carefully pulling one of the doors open just enough for him to slip outside undetected. Sneaking out and around the place was one of the few times his very thin and light frame came to his advantage.

He took careful, deliberate steps towards the odd device, treating it as if it were some sort of foreign object that'd manage to find its way onto the island.

Before long, he was slowly reaching for the machine, seizing it between his fingers and gently running his fingers over the buttons.

Now that he was able to get a closer look at it, he noticed that some of the buttons had some sort of relation to time. 'Min'… 'hour'… 'day'… 'year'…

…And that there was also a massive 'travel' button right at the bottom centre.

Paper couldn't help but stare at the device for a few moments. If his assumptions were correct, and he really could enter any time he wanted into this machine and travel to that time…

…Would that make it some sort of… time machine?

Paper took another look at the device, gently biting his lip. His eyes were practically glued to that oh-so-tempting travel button, as if just the sight alone was just begging him to attempt to visit another time… perhaps even his future?

He shouldn't. He really shouldn't. What if this was some sort of trap - an elaborate prank on him set by Cherries? Or the machine was unfinished and completely screwed over where and when he'd end up?

But then again… what if it worked? What if he was able to get a glance at where he'd end up later in life? To see how the hotel develops from here?

Perhaps he'd even be able to look at his and OJ's future… to see if there was even a possibility of them becoming a thing? To know if he even truly had a chance of his feelings being reciprocated? Perhaps he'd see things he'd only been able to witness in his wildest dreams… the two of them sharing a kiss, or cuddling, maybe even whispering sweet affirmations to one another and sharing pet names…

…But what if he instead saw things he'd dreaded most in his most horrible nightmares? What if OJ hated him for his feelings and decided to stop being friends with him altogether? Or even worse - what if he ended up with Salt?

Paper took another close look at the small little hourglass display as those thoughts raced around his brain.

…It didn't really matter if his future was incredible or devastating… he would have to face whatever was destined to happen to him eventually anyway…

…Was there really any harm in taking a peek into the future? One small, brief little peek?

He'd never know what could have been if he didn't try.

Just one look. A fleeting glance. For perhaps only a few seconds.

That's all it would take.

Before he could second-guess himself any further, he fiddled with the buttons on the calculator and ensured that (if the machine actually worked properly in the first place) it'd send him around nine years into the future.

He hit 'travel' and shut his eyes, bracing himself for whatever potentially life-altering event would follow.

-

Paper wasn't entirely sure what had happened next. A part of him expected absolutely nothing to happen, entirely prepared for the faint buzz of Cherries' hushed snickers to hit his ears as it was revealed this was all a part of his latest prank.

But to his surprise… he felt himself be sucked up by some sort of… wormhole, perhaps?

"Aah!-"

And in a fraction of a second, he'd opened his eyes to find himself gracefully floating down onto a patch of uneven grass, landing on it with a quiet puff.

Soon after that, he pushed himself back onto his feet, taking the opportunity to glance around at wherever he was. Or perhaps whenever he was.

He wasn't entirely sure how to explain the sensation, but the air around him appeared to be more… crisp? It felt as if it were much easier to breathe in this new environment, and it was like every wisp of air that he inhaled truly smelt of the earth below him. Every blade of grass he came into contact with as he wandered across the field he'd landed in seemed so much softer. So much more alive.

The first thing he recognised as he walked was the Crappy Cliff in the distance, which he decided it'd probably be a good idea to walk towards. He'd likely be able to locate the hotel from there.

But the second he got to the cliff itself, looking back at where the hotel was supposed to be…

…Paper saw a completely different building standing there instead. It was much less… abnormal, being a more modernistic grey building simply labelled 'HOOT'.

What could that possibly mean? Was it some kind of replacement for the hotel? A different building entirely? Were all the contestants all inside?

He took tentative steps towards the mysterious building - glancing around to see if he'd be able to spot anyone on the way - but it seemed like no other objects were around at all.

Eventually, he reached the front door of the odd building, hiding the time machine behind his back and gently knocking on the door as he awaited a response.

"Oh- coming!"

He felt himself freeze up as he recognised that muffled voice through the door, hearing footsteps approaching him from a distance.

It was… maybe a little lower in pitch, yes, but the sound was unmistakable.

That voice was his own.

The door then flung open, and once again he heard the voice, speaking in a practiced pace that he was all too familiar to adapting whenever he'd have to cover for OJ back at the hotel.

"Sorry, the OSC isn't here right now, but I'm sure that I can-"

His words were cut off when their eyes met, and just as he'd imagined, Paper saw what appeared to be his future self.

All things considered, the two of them weren't that different; his future version was just a bit taller and had a slightly wider frame, as well as faded baby blue horizontal lines and a much lighter margin.

Though the biggest difference between them was the glistening silver ring on the older one's left hand, practically taunting the younger Paper with a billion questions that whizzed across his mind too fast for him to fully grasp any of them.

For a few agonisingly long seconds, the two of them just stood there staring at each other, examining their counterpart's appearance with awestruck eyes.

But the older version of Paper's lips soon curled into a thin grin, chuckling quietly to himself as he spoke.

"…Ah. So today's the day, huh?"

The shorter sheet blinked at him in mild, innocent confusion, tiling his head as he spoke up.

"Wait… what? What do you mean by that?"

"I know exactly what you're doing here. I've been you, remember?"

Paper let out a quiet hum as he processed that piece of information.

If this future version of himself remembers this exact moment… then maybe his visit to the future was planned out by fate all along? Perhaps he had nothing to worry about in terms of space-time.

Just as he was about to ask his future self another question, the older Paper carefully moved past him and left the building, shutting the door behind him and uttering one last sentence before he rushed off to who knows where.

"Just wait here, I'll send the man you've come to see your way now."

And just like that, he was off, leaving Paper standing outside the door of the building and completely in the dark as to what was going on.

What was his older self on about? Why did he leave so suddenly? Was that ring around his finger really a sign that maybe-

"Uhh… hello?"

Paper jumped at the sound of a slightly deeper voice from behind him, spinning around with a quiet squeak as he locked eyes with whoever had approached him.

And to his surprise… he was met with none other than OJ.

As if it were some sort of reflex reaction, his heart began to beat at a million miles a minute. Seeing an older, more mature version of the man he'd grown to love over the years up close out of the blue was absolutely not for the weak. His glass somehow seemed so much smoother, sleeker, cleaner… and the patient, curious smile carved onto his face certainly didn't help the situation at all.

Paper could only stare at him for a few precious seconds - hoping that the heat rising to his cheeks wasn't all that visible on his face - until his brain finally kicked into gear and he realised that OJ was probably expecting a response from him; one he tried his absolute best to make sound as coherent as possible.

"Ah- oh- hiya!"

OJ chuckled to himself at his frantic stuttering, clearing his throat as he spoke again.

"Sorry, I didn't mean to startle you. I'm OJ, one of the members of the OSC that leads our society here. Paper told me you're one of the new MeAfterLife arrivals? Notebook Sheet, right?"

Paper blinked to himself as he took a moment to comprehend what this future version of the hotel manager he'd come to know was telling him.

Notebook Sheet? Was that some sort of alibi his older self had given him? What was this OSC OJ spoke of? Was it a replacement for the hotel? What was going on?

Every second he spent here, he gained a plethora of new questions and absolutely no answers. Perhaps this really was a mistake.

Still, he knew that he probably didn't have any other option but to roll with whatever was going on. He'd rather not risk OJ finding out who he actually was, after all.

"Oh, yes- that's me!"

OJ nodded along to his words, resting a hand on the handle of one of the 'HOOT' building's doors as he spoke again.

"Right, well, if you'll just follow me inside, we can get you registered and find out what your role in our society will be!"

Despite OJ's cheerful, reassuring words, Paper's feet didn't move an inch from where they were planted in the ground. In fact, he seemed almost paralysed, his entire body completely frozen as his eyes fixated on one specific item on the hand OJ had rested on the handle.

A ring.

A glistening gold ring with a delicately cut amber right in the centre.

A ring that just so happened to perfectly match the opal ring he'd seen on his future self's hand a minute ago…

The question slipped out of his mouth before he could even register it, the words sounding distant, astonished and profound all at once.

"That ring… are you…?-"

OJ let out a quiet hum as he looked back at Paper, before his eyes wandered onto the aforementioned ring, chuckling to himself as he nodded, a prideful smirk sliding onto his face as he spoke.

"Married? Yep. To the very same guy you spoke to just a few seconds ago, actually. Paper! I couldn't ask for a better partner to share my life with…"

And just like that, Paper's heart completely stopped.

In the future, he was OJ's husband.

The gears in his head were suddenly spinning far too fast, and yet nowhere near fast enough; it felt like his entire body had been set ablaze in an instant.

Of course those rings had practically confirmed his suspicions - his hopes - but to actually hear those words fall so gracefully from OJ's lips, in a voice far smoother than silk?

That made them feel so much more… real. Much more valid, and undeniable, the weight of the words wrapping Paper in a thick blanket of reassurance and euphoria that he'd never be able to put into words.

"Y-You… you love him? Really?"

It was all he could force out of his throat when his thoughts had finally calmed down enough for him to stumble through a sentence. OJ nodded along without hesitation.

"Is that even a question? Of course I do! He's the most sweet, adorable, compassionate and brave object I've ever met. He's always been there for me, showing me nothing but warmth and kindness and never once thinking of abandoning me; even when things between us got rough and I was being an absolute jerk to him. He's so passionate about all the little things he loves, he always goes the extra mile to make sure everyone he knows is accounted for, and god- he's just so- so perfect…"

Paper could only watch in sheer astonishment as OJ let out a deep, dreamy sigh of relief, a dopey smile falling onto his face as his eyes wandered off into no direction in particular.

He could barely believe his ears. Had OJ really just said all of that? About him? Surely he couldn't mean all of that, right?

And yet… his tone has been overflowing with genuine admiration and reverence, each and every word clearly spoken directly from his heart.

Paper never could've imagined that OJ could genuinely, truly love him back in such a deep, meaningful way, noticing all the little things he'd done for the two of them across their lives…

He could already tell that the memory of OJ calling him 'perfect' was going to pleasantly plague his dreams for many days to come.

Moments later, OJ seemed to snap back to reality, clearing his throat as he spoke again.

"Uh- sorry, I launched into a bit of a ramble there… let me actually get you registered and all-"

"Oh! No, uhm, it's- it's fine, actually…"

OJ raised an eyebrow at Paper's declaration, tilting his head and trying to make sense of his words.

Paper's mind was sent into a brief frenzy, trying to think of some sort of excuse to avoid whatever this 'register' process was…

"…Can you tell me more about… how you and Paper got together first? Uh- only if you're comfortable sharing, of course! It uhm- I just- I don't know, it seems so… interesting? Haha…"

Oh, he was just digging himself deeper and deeper into this hole, wasn't he.

Out of all the things he could've said, why would he pick that?

Deep down he knew why, of course, but it was such a weird question to ask, wasn't it? Why couldn't he just control his curiosity? This OJ probably thought he was such a creep by now, snooping around into other people's personal lives when he seemingly hardly even knew them at all-

"Oh, sure! I don't mind. It was an incredible day, actually. I invited him out to a boat - the same one we'd gotten on in a challenge together in the first season of the show - gathered up every ounce of my courage, and confessed to him right then and there. I sorta just… rambled on about everything I felt towards him, and to my surprise, he reciprocated those feelings. We stayed on that boat for the entire rest of the day after that, watching the sunset wrapped up in each other's arms."

Paper felt every part of his chest light up with that answer, completely blown away by what he'd just heard. Was OJ really so open and proud about their relationship that he was willing to recount that clearly warm and precious moment between them at the drop of a hat? Was he really, truly happy as Paper's partner?

All of what he was learning right now was absolutely blowing him away. He wasn't sure how much more of this he could take, if he was being honest. Everything about his and OJ's relationship in the future just seemed so… unbelievable. So comforting, and safe, and magical…

Despite the fact that he still had absolutely no clue what else was going on in this future, or where their hotel had gone, and where all the other residents were…

…Maybe he didn't need to think about any of that. Maybe he'd just figure all of those details along the way instead.

The sight of Paper's future self approaching the two of them from the distance was enough to lead him out of his train of thought, the older sheet of Paper quickly making his way to OJ's side as he spoke up.

"Oh, OJ, Suitcase said she needs you back over to help with the Magnet situation."

OJ let out a slightly annoyed groan at that, taking a glance at the sheet he'd been talking to, but the older Paper only shook his head and gestured over to some random objects who's silhouettes the smaller Paper could barely even see in the distance.

"Don't worry about Notebook Sheet, I'll take care of things here! Just head over to where everyone else is, they really need your help!"

"I… okay. I'll head over. Thanks hun."

OJ gave his Paper a quick kiss on the cheek before heading off, waving him goodbye with an appreciative smile.

Once he was certain that they were out of OJ's view, the older Paper looked back at his younger counterpart, reaching for the arm that was still sitting behind his back and pulling it and the time machine forward.

"That's your cue to go, mini-me!"

Paper looked down at the device in his hand for a moment, his hands trembling ever so slightly as he let everything that had just occurred sink into his mind.

His eyes snapped back up to his older counterpart, biting the inside of his cheek before he asked one last question.

"I… I've learned so much today… and- and you said you've been me before, so… what do I- what do I do about all this? I don't want to lose this future… I don't want to lose… him…"

Paper felt his future self place a reassuring hand on his shoulder, sighing quietly to himself with a comforting and gentle smile.

"…That's the hard part. You do… nothing. Even with everything you know now… you can't rush this stuff and try to control your fate, or alter your future. That's just not how it works. You've just gotta wait 'till that dumbass we love so much confesses to you on that boat. You've just gotta let things… happen. It won't be all sunshine and rainbows, yeah, but… you'll get to the future you want, trust me. You just need to be patient."

Paper let out a deep sigh, nodding along to his future self's words as he looked down at the machine in his hands once more.

At the end of the day, he know that the older Paper was right. There would be no point in trying to change his past or future. What was the use in worrying about the what-ifs and the maybes?

Maybe… he should instead just live in the present. Keep the knowledge he'd gained as a token of hope. Hope that there would indeed be a light at the end of this confusing, twisted tunnel.

Paper took in one last deep breath, giving his future self a determined nod as he entered in the date for his own time and hit 'travel' once more.

Because now he could be absolutely certain that OJ cared about him. Now he could be certain that their bond really was as special as his heart had told him it'd was as the years went by, and that OJ truly did see the worth and value in their bond just as deeply as he did. Whether it was platonic or romantic… perhaps that wasn't what really mattered in the end.

He'd gladly wait as long as it takes for OJ to realise that too. For OJ to confess that their connection runs deeper than being acquaintances forced together by their proximity… and for them to one day become partners in every aspect of their lives.

-

"And then I was sent back to 2018… and stuff continued as normal. You'd luckily been in your office all day and nobody else had noticed I was gone, so… I was kind of able to get away with everything undetected. I never really messed with time again after that."

OJ could barely believe what his husband was telling him right now. Paper had finally finished explaining his experience with time travel and he felt like even more of an idiot than he had half an hour ago.

He let the silence settle over them for a few seconds, processing everything he'd been told from the comfort of their bed, when a realisation snapped into place in his mind.

"Wait wait wait- that- Notebook Sheet was actually you?-"

Paper couldn't help but burst into manic giggles, nodding along as he struggled to keep his composure, shuffling even closer to OJ as he spoke.

"Wha- yes! You seriously didn't know? I thought I was being so obvious! Especially since now I know you've also used this device before."

"I was absolutely clueless..."

Paper allowed his chuckles to die down after a while, moving to wrap his arms around OJ's glass.

"So you really described our relationship in such detail to someone you barely knew?"

OJ let out an offended scoff, and Paper could feel his glass start to heat up in embarrassment under his arms.

"Wha- in my defense, you did basically the same thing!"

"I knew 'CJ' was you, though! You didn't know Sheet was me!"

OJ let out a couple almost offended sputters that melted away into saddened gruffs, sighing into his lover's shoulder.

"…I can't believe we both knew for sure that we were gonna get married… and it still took us practically a decade to actually tie the knot."

Paper gently stroked a hand along the back of OJ's glass, his smile only growing even wider as he thought back on everything the two of them had been through.

Their relationship had certainly been… a bit of a mess, even outside of the time-travel shenanigans. But it was their mess. A magnificent, marvellous mess.

Their journey here was complex, convoluted, and filled to the brim with ups and downs… but after all of it, they'd finally found themselves here. And these precious moments, this love they shared… this was something he wouldn't even consider giving up for everything else in the entire universe.

"Well, you know what they say, 'Jay… good things come to those who wait."
